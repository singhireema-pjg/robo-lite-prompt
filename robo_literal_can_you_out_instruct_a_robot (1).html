Cheat Sheet

📖 The Literal-Bot Guide

AI doesn't have a human brain or common sense. Whenever you give instructions to an AI step-by-step, remember these golden rules:

🟢 Rule 1: No Common Sense

The robot never fills in missing steps. If you skip opening a jar, it tries to scoop through the lid.

🟢 Rule 2: Literal Meanings

Words matter! "Put peanut butter on the bread" places the whole jar on the bread unless you specify using a knife.

🟢 Rule 3: Strict Order

Steps are executed exactly in the order you type them. You can't spread peanut butter before picking up the knife.
